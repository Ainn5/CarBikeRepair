How to request help

1. Call a provider

Say whether you have a car, bike or scooter and describe the problem.

2. Share your location

Send a nearby landmark or map pin, plus vehicle make and model.

3. Confirm before dispatch

Agree on arrival estimate, call-out fee, repair price and towing destination before work starts.

Phone numbers, operating hours and coverage may change. Listings are provided for convenience; Ainn does not guarantee third-party service quality or availability. If you are in immediate danger, move to a safe place and contact the appropriate local emergency service.
